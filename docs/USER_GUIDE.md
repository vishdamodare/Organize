# Vyapaarsathi — User Guide

Vyapaarsathi keeps your customers, enquiries, follow-ups, quotations and orders in one place.

## Getting started (owner)

1. Sign in with the owner username and password you received. Change your password: top-right menu → Preferences.
2. **Settings** (left menu): enter company name, address, phone, email, GSTIN, default tax % and your quotation terms. Logo: Administration → Branding.
3. **Team** → Create User: enter name, username, email, password; under *Roles* choose **Salesperson**. To remove access later, untick **Is Active** — the person is signed out immediately.
4. **Import your customers**: Overview → *Download CSV template*, fill it in Excel/Sheets, save as CSV, then *Import customers*. You'll match your columns to CRM fields, see a preview, and after import a summary with any rows that had errors (nothing is skipped silently; you can also undo an import).

## Daily work

**Overview** shows customers, new enquiries, open deals, today's and overdue follow-ups, won deals and order value for Today / This week / This month / Custom. Click a box to open the list.

**Customers** — *New Customer*. If the phone/email/name already exists you'll get a duplicate warning. The customer page shows details on the left, and the owner, next follow-up and last interaction on the right; tabs below show the timeline, enquiries & deals, quotations & orders.

**Enquiries** — record what the customer wants (category, metal, purity, gemstone, budget, dates, photos). When it becomes a real opportunity, open it and choose **⋯ → Convert to Deal**.

**Sales Pipeline** — drag deal cards between stages. To mark a deal Lost, open it, set the stage to Lost and pick a reason. *Won does not create an order* — orders come from accepted quotations.

**Tasks & Follow-ups** — schedule calls, meetings and follow-ups linked to a customer, deal or quotation. Use the filters *Due Today*, *Overdue*, *Upcoming*, *Completed*. Mark done by setting status Completed.

**Products** — your catalogue with indicative prices (reference only; not stock, not live gold rates).

**Quotations** — *New Quotation* → choose the customer → save → in **Line Items** click **+**: pick a product (name and price fill in) or type a description, quantity (grams allowed, e.g. 12.5), unit price, discount % and tax %. Totals are calculated automatically. Print: **⋯ → Print to PDF**. Set status to Sent / Accepted / Rejected / Expired. Lines can't be changed after Accepted.

**Orders** (owner) — open an **Accepted** quotation → **⋯ → Convert to Order**. The lines are copied; the quotation is then locked. Move the order through Confirmed → In Progress → Ready for Delivery → Delivered (or Cancelled). Lines can be edited only while the order is Draft.

**Reports** (owner) — enquiries by source/status, deals by stage, won vs lost, lost reasons, quotations, orders, sales over time, salesperson performance. *Export CSV* downloads what you see.

## What the numbers mean

- **Order value** = total of orders dated in the period, except Draft and Cancelled. It is *not* money received.
- **Won deal value** = expected value of deals marked Won in the period.
- **Pipeline value** = expected value of open deals.

## Salespeople

You see all company customers and deals, and can edit those assigned to you. You see only your own tasks. You can create quotations; the owner converts them to orders.
